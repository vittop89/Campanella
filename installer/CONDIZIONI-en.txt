CAMPANELLA - TERMS OF USE
Read once. It is short on purpose.


1. WHAT IT IS

Campanella is a personal tool that prepares text: the Google Apps Script code
to paste into your account, the folders of a school year, the timetable
emails (which are sent only to you) and your own timetable in your calendar.
It never touches your mail, your calendar or your Drive on its own: you are
always the one pressing Run. It sends no data to anyone, collects no
statistics and needs no account. It connects to the internet only when you
ask it to: to check for updates or to download rizzo-pii.

For Google Forms it prepares a second script, to be pasted inside the form:
every year it creates the response sheet, links the form to it and closes the
form at the end of the year. It is a separate project with its own
permissions, and you are the one who runs it. Without the optional manifest
that Campanella prepares with the code, Google gives it permission on all
the forms of your account; with the manifest, only on that form. Either way
it opens only the form it lives in. It sends no email, shares nothing and
deletes nothing: it clears last year responses from the form only if you
ask, and only after checking they are already in an older sheet.

With more than one form, instead of the script inside each form you can use
a control sheet: a Google Sheet with one row per form and a script inside it,
which does the same work for every form listed in the sheet. Since it opens
forms that live outside the sheet, it asks for permission on all the forms
of your account, as well as on your sheets and your Drive; it then opens only
the forms listed. You paste it and run it yourself too, but the closings it
schedules run on their own, on the day you set, without you pressing Run. It
sends no email, shares nothing, and clears responses from a form only on the
conditions described above.

It runs on Windows with a Google Workspace account (Gmail, Drive, Calendar):
with Microsoft 365 it does nothing. The staff list can be read from the
ClasseViva register by Spaggiari; with other registers it must be pasted by
hand or requested from the school office. The timetable file is the one
produced by Orario Facile, or a teacher/day/period table. The interface is
in Italian only.


2. THE DATA YOU WILL PROCESS IS NOT YOURS

Names, work addresses, timetables, correspondence with colleagues and
families are personal data whose controller is the school, not you. The
GDPR household exemption does not cover professional activity.

In practice:

  - The staff list and the timetables with surnames stay on the computer
    where you use Campanella. From Settings you can keep them in a file
    inside the school Drive instead of next to the program: this is the
    recommended choice, because they then stay within the institutional
    account.

  - Better NOT to bring work mail and documents onto a personal computer.
    If you really need them, take as little as possible.

  - Always leave students' data out: certifications, individual education
    plans, clinical reports, referrals. They are special categories of data
    (Article 9 GDPR) and must not be copied locally.

  - The answers to your Google Forms are data about students and families.
    They stay in the form and in the sheet inside the school Drive; the
    sheet the script creates is yours and nobody else sees it until you
    share it. Sheets from past years do not expire by themselves either.

  - Delete what you no longer need: a file on disk does not expire by
    itself.

  - If the computer is not yours alone, or is not encrypted, the problem
    multiplies. A lost USB stick is a data breach that must be reported.


3. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE: ANONYMISE FIRST

When you paste a document into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or similar, that text
leaves your computer and ends up on servers you do not control, where it may
be retained. If it contains personal data of third parties, that is a
transfer to an external party which, for a personal initiative, has no legal
basis.

That is why Campanella includes the Privacy tool: remove the personal data
locally, give the assistant only the placeholders, and put the real names
back into the answer when it comes back.

Two things remain true even so:

  - No automatic recogniser catches 100%. Re-read the cleaned text before
    pasting it: the final check is your responsibility.

  - Anonymising is not always enough. In a class of twenty, context
    re-identifies: "the support teacher of class 3B" contains no names and
    identifies perfectly well.


4. WHAT YOU DECIDE

The application always shows you beforehand what is about to happen, starts
in trial mode and never writes to anyone else: the timetable emails go only
to you. But the decisions are yours: what to keep on the computer, what to
paste into an assistant, how long to retain it.

You are the one who must comply with the GDPR, your school's regulations and
the instructions of the Data Protection Officer (DPO). The law does not
require you to ask permission to tidy up your own mail inside the school
account, but your school's instructions may: Campanella gives you a
technical note and an email template to inform the principal and the DPO,
if you wish or if it is required.
